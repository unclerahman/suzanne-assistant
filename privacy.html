Privacy Policy — Suzanne Assistant

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who uses this app

Suzanne Assistant is a private tool used only by its owner, Dr Abdul Rahman, with his own Google accounts. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the Google Calendar API to read events and to create, update or delete events on the owner's calendars, in order to check his availability and keep his schedule up to date. Changes are made only on the owner's instruction or with his approval.

How the data is used and stored

  • Calendar data is used only to provide scheduling help to the owner.
  • Access tokens and any cached schedule data are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

Suzanne Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Locally stored tokens and cached data are deleted when access is removed.
